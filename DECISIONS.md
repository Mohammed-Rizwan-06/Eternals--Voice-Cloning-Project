# Established decisions

- Live managed-call protection is the product; upload classification is not.
- Twilio Programmable Voice + Media Streams is preferred; WebRTC then labelled microphone simulation are fallbacks.
- Official AASIST is the primary authenticity candidate; a verified Transformers classifier is backup.
- Authenticity, transcription, scam intelligence, caller intelligence, and fusion remain independent.
- SSE carries server updates; REST carries commands.
- Raw audio and full transcripts are not retained by default.
- Normal mode uses unavailable adapters. Demo Mode is deliberate, deterministic, and labelled.
- Rules are explainable/configurable prototype assumptions; model confidence is not accuracy.
- Untrusted pickle/joblib artifacts are prohibited.
- `Srv99x/voice-detection-ai` is reference/scaffold only under its licence; unsafe artifacts, random UI, claims, and upload-first flow are rejected.
- No claim of Truecaller, teammate, Twilio, model, bank, telecom, production, or universal-language integration without verified evidence.
